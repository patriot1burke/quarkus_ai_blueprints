# Quarkus AI Community

Quarkus AI is a combination of many different open source projects.

* [LangChain4j](https://github.com/langchain4j/langchain4j)
* [Quarkus LangChain4j](https://github.com/quarkiverse/quarkus-langchain4j) Integrates with LC4J and provides additional Quarkus features.
* [Quarkus Core](https://github.com/quarkusio/quarkus)
* [The Quarkiverse](https://github.com/quarkiverse) Hundreds of additional extensions for Quarkus.
* [Quarkus Flow](https://github.com/quarkiverse/quarkus-flow) Pure Java workflow engine integratable with Agentic Services. 
* [LangFuse](https://langfuse.com/) Popular open source service for monitoring and evaluating AI applications.
